# Security, Privacy and Sensitive Knowledge

## Public repository boundary

This repository is public. Do not commit:

- passwords, API keys, tokens or private URLs;
- raw conversation exports;
- personal identifiers or private reflections;
- unpublished commercial strategy;
- private health, biometric or contemplative data;
- restricted Indigenous, ancestral, sacred or community knowledge;
- third-party assets without redistribution rights.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue containing a secret, personal data or restricted cultural material. Contact the repository owner privately through the account contact method available on GitHub.

## Sensor and contemplative modules

Sensor integrations remain disabled until they have:

- explicit informed consent;
- data-minimization and retention rules;
- signal-quality and failure behavior;
- privacy and security review;
- non-medical disclosures;
- immediate pause, stop and exit behavior.

## Supply chain

Before adding a dependency:

- justify why it is required;
- review maintenance and license status;
- avoid packages that duplicate existing capability;
- pin or lock versions through the chosen package manager;
- record any runtime permissions or external services.

## Release boundary

No deployment, publication pipeline or production secret may be introduced without explicit project-owner review. Deployment requires the exact phrase `APROBAR DEPLOY`.
