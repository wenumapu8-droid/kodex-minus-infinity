## Visual rules

- Vertical 9:16 always. 1080×1920 export.
- Safe area: top 250px (avatar/IG UI), bottom 280px (caption/reactions). Keep critical content in middle 1080×1390.
- Palette: same as posts — obsidian black, bone cream, bronze, ember, silver. No story-default neon stickers.
- Motion: subtle — no fast cuts, no spinning text, no autoplay sound spikes.
- Font for overlays: DM Serif Display for hero text, Inter for caption metadata. Native IG sans only if no other option.

---

## Forbidden

- Stock-photo gradients, default sticker explosion, "swipe up to slay", emojis as decoration, perfume-ad copy, "ancient wisdom" / "tribal vibes" / "spiritual journey".
- No music with vocals over piece-detail frames.
