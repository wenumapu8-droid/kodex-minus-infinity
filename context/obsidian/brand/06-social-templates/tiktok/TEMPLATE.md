## Audio rules

- Natural sound (hammer, file, water cooling) > licensed music. Use sound for craft videos.
- If music: low BPM, instrumental, no vocals over visual peaks.
- Forbidden: trending pop with explicit lyrics, "epic" cinematic stings, royalty-free spa flute.

---

## Visual rules

- 9:16 always. Native vertical capture, not cropped landscape.
- Safe area: top 220px (TikTok UI), bottom 480px (caption + UI). Critical content fits within central 1080×1220.
- Light: ritual altar style — low key, side-lit, bronze/cream/black palette. No mall-light fluorescents.
- Stabilization: tripod or gimbal. No handheld shake unless it's a stylistic choice for one frame.

---

## Forbidden

- Trend hijacks unrelated to craft (no point/dancing/lip-sync trends).
- Captions in trending fonts; use sober sans (closest TikTok default), or no caption.
- "Wenu Mapu" pronounced wrong by AI voiceover. If voiceover, must be human.

---

## Accessibility

- Burn captions onto every video (TikTok auto-captions are good but verify).
- Alt text in caption description for screen readers (one factual sentence).
