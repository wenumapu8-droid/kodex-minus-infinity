## Capa fundacional — "Somos uno" (cosmopolita)

Reescrito bajo [[filosofia-cosmica-somos-uno-2026-06-23]]. Lo que cambia respecto del primer draft:

- Las 10 colecciones ya **no son catálogos sueltos**: son **caras de una misma verdad** — caminos distintos que suben al mismo cielo (*wenu mapu*, la tierra de arriba).
- Posicionamiento: **adorno ritual cosmopolita** — de todos los pueblos y todos los cielos, anclado en raíz mapuche (idioma-hogar).
- Tono: **reverente, sereno, perenne (zen/tao) + terroso ancestral.** No new-age hueco.
- **Frase-semilla** (hilo conductor, editable): *"Different hands, different lands — the same sky."*
- **Guardrail (clave):** reverencia, NO apropiación de disfraz. Cada linaje se **nombra y acredita** con respeto (India = devoción; Maya = astronomía; etc.). "Somos uno" es **humildad**, no aplanar lo sagrado. Ante la duda: menos afirmación, más reverencia.

> **Esto resuelve la bandera anterior de India/Maya.** Ya no son "nombres externos que diluyen": bajo la tesis cosmopolita son linajes honrados como caras de la misma búsqueda — *siempre que* el copy los acredite y diga honestamente que son **curados, no forjados**. Así quedan escritos. (El naming sigue sujeto a tu OK final.)

## El arco (las 10 como una sola historia)

Un solo viaje: del origen al cosmos, del oficio a la materia viva, y el puente entre pueblos — todos mirando hacia arriba.

1. **Origin** — el lenguaje universal hablado en su lengua madre (mapudungun).
2. **Araucanía** — la **tierra** particular: el Wallmapu, raíz del fundador.
3. **Atacama** — el **cosmos** tocando el cuerpo: el meteorito caído.
4. **Solar** — el **cosmos** de día: antü, el sol que sale para todos.
5. **Fossil** — el **tiempo profundo**: el suelo común bajo todos los pueblos.
6. **Organic** — la **materia viva**: madera, hueso, asta, semilla.
7. **Ornamental** — el **oficio**: la superficie trabajada en toda cultura.
8. **Neo** — la **vanguardia**: lo ancestral empujado al presente (trance/festival).
9. **India** — el **puente** (curada, acreditada): adorno como devoción.
10. **Maya** — el **puente** (curada, acreditada): el cielo, escrito.

> Eje editorial: **origen → tierra → cosmos → tiempo/materia → oficio → vanguardia → puente.** Cada texto deja un hilo que el siguiente recoge, y todos vuelven al mismo cielo.

## 1 · ORIGIN

**slug:** `origin`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Where the language begins
**Subtitle (EN):** Forge · Form · First light
**Storytelling (EN):**

> Every people that ever adorned the body was reaching for the same thing — to mark what is sacred, to look up. Origin is where Wenu Mapu speaks that universal language in its mother tongue: the ritual ring, the ear weight, the forms Ocin shapes at the forge in titanium, silver and bronze, along the Mapuche path of platería. Slow metalwork as ceremony, not decoration. This is the core line, the ground the others rise from. Different hands, different lands — the same sky. Here, it begins in Mapudungun.

*Nota interna:* línea forjada de autor → "forge / hand-forged" honesto. Establece la tesis cosmopolita y el mapudungun como idioma-hogar.

## 2 · ATACAMA

**slug:** `atacama`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Stone that fell from the sky
**Subtitle (EN):** Desert · Sky · Meteorite
**Storytelling (EN):**

> Four and a half billion years before the desert, this was sky. Atacama sets genuine Vacamuerta meteorite — cosmic iron fallen into Chile's driest land — into hand-forged silver, each ring numbered, each existing only once. Ocin forges these himself: the founder's hands against matter older than the Earth. The same vault every people has watched once held this stone. To wear it is to carry a fragment of that shared sky against the skin — proof that what is above and what is worn on the body were never two separate things.

*Nota interna:* serie de autor real (Ritual Ring Vacamuerta). Hand-forged + meteorito real + numerado = verdadero. Unidad ("the same vault every people has watched").

## 3 · ARAUCANÍA

**slug:** `araucania`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Rooted in the Wallmapu
**Subtitle (EN):** Forest · Volcano · Pewen
**Storytelling (EN):**

> Every path toward the sky begins in some particular earth. For Wenu Mapu that ground is the Wallmapu — the Mapuche territory of southern Chile, where Ocin's craft took root before it crossed the world. Araucanía draws from it: the pewen over the volcanic line, the dark soil, the long patience of the araucaria that outlives generations. This is the home-land of the whole story, named and honored as itself — not the costume of a culture, but the founder's own roots, the particular tierra from which the universal is spoken.

*Nota interna:* ancla el origen REAL en Chile/Wallmapu (no California). "Named and honored as itself" + "not the costume of a culture" = guardrail de reverencia.

## 4 · SOLAR

**slug:** `solar`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Antü — the sun
**Subtitle (EN):** Sun · Gold · Day
**Storytelling (EN):**

> One sun rises over every people. The Mapuche call it antü — the father of heat, life and wisdom, the eight-point star at the heart of the kultrún — yet Egypt, the Andes and Bali all turned the same way at dawn. Solar holds that shared warmth: radiant geometry, warm metals, pieces made to catch the day. It is the line worn as the ceremony turns toward first light — We Tripantu, the sun at the year's beginning. Light you don't reflect so much as carry. A small sun held against the body.

*Nota interna:* antü canónico (Canio & Pozo). Nombra varios linajes solares con reverencia (no exotismo). We Tripantu = año nuevo mapuche.

## 5 · NEO

**slug:** `neo`
**Eyebrow (EN):** The forward edge
**Subtitle (EN):** New · Graphic · Sharper
**Storytelling (EN):**

> The ancestral is the avant-garde. Neo carries the oldest impulse — to adorn the body, to look up — into sharper, cleaner, more graphic form. Nothing here chases a trend; it brings one cosmology forward into the present. These are pieces for the contemporary ritual: the dance floor as ceremonial ground, trance as a modern doorway to the same place every ancestor walked toward. Old root, new edge. Proof that being truly rooted is the most modern thing a piece can be.

*Nota interna:* tesis de [[norte-de-marca-2026-06-21]] ("lo ancestral ES la vanguardia") + mundo trance/psytrance como ritual contemporáneo + unidad ("the same place every ancestor walked toward").

## 6 · ORNAMENTAL

**slug:** `ornamental`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Where detail is the rite
**Subtitle (EN):** Filigree · Greca · Worked surface
**Storytelling (EN):**

> Wherever people have worked metal by hand, the surface fills with meaning — the Mapuche greca, the filigree of a dozen traditions, a line repeated until it becomes a kind of prayer. Ornamental is the line where the detail is the ritual: dense, worked surface that rewards the slow eye and the close look. This is patience made visible, the opposite of mass-made smoothness. Adornment meant to be read, not just seen — the shared human habit of writing the sacred into what we wear.

*Nota interna:* oficio universal. No afirma que cada pieza es hand-forged (mezcla de orígenes); foco en "detalle = rito" y en el gesto compartido entre culturas.

## 7 · ORGANIC

**slug:** `organic`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Grown, not cast
**Subtitle (EN):** Wood · Bone · Horn · Seed
**Storytelling (EN):**

> Before metal, the living material. Organic gathers wood, bone, horn and seed — things that were once alive and still remember it. Walnut warmed by the body, a seed worn smooth, weight drawn from the earth instead of the forge. In stretching traditions across every continent, natural matter is the oldest way to mark the body's passage. These pieces grow heavier and more personal with wear, taking the shape of whoever carries them. Matter that ages with you — küyen-slow and cyclical, the way the moon returns for everyone.

*Nota interna:* "gathers" (no "handmade") cubre mezcla hand-forged/curated. küyen (luna/ciclos) cerrando en clave de unidad ("returns for everyone").

---

## 8 · FOSSIL

**slug:** `fossil`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Deep time worn close
**Subtitle (EN):** Amber · Ammonite · Stone
**Storytelling (EN):**

> Some matter remembers longer than any people. Fossil sets amber that trapped a forest's afternoon, ammonite coiled before the dinosaurs, stone that holds the press of deep time. To wear it is to carry the planet's own memory against the pulse — older than every culture that ever looked up, the ground all of them stood on. Atacama brought the cosmos to the body; Fossil brings the Earth's ancient archive. One sky above, one deep time below, and the body held between them.

*Nota interna:* cierra el arco materia/memoria, referencia explícita a Atacama (coherencia de las 10) y a la unidad ("the ground all of them stood on").

## 9 · INDIA

**slug:** `india`
**Eyebrow (EN):** Ornament as devotion
**Subtitle (EN):** Ornament · Ceremony · Devotion
**Storytelling (EN):**

> In the Indian subcontinent, adornment has long been devotion — the body dressed for the sacred, ornament worn as prayer made visible. India honors that lineage: dense, ceremonial pieces curated for the way they carry the same impulse Wenu Mapu speaks in Mapudungun. They are gathered and selected with respect, not forged in our studio, and named for the tradition they come from rather than dressed up as our own. One of the world's oldest ornament languages — a different hand, a different land, reaching toward the same sky.

*Nota interna:* ⚠️ **curada/importada**, NO forjada. Copy lo dice ("curated… not forged in our studio") y **acredita el linaje** (devoción india) en vez de usarlo como estética exótica. Guardrail de [[filosofia-cosmica-somos-uno-2026-06-23]] cumplido. **Pendiente: confirmar el nombre "India" con Ocin antes del launch.**

## 10 · MAYA

**slug:** `maya`
**Eyebrow (EN):** The sky, written
**Subtitle (EN):** Glyph · Geometry · Cosmos
**Storytelling (EN):**

> The Maya read the sky with a precision few civilizations have matched, and wrote what they saw into glyph and geometry carried on the body. Maya honors that astronomy: symbol worn as adornment, cosmic geometry curated for its language rather than forged in our studio. They named the same stars the Mapuche call wanglen — another people, another tongue, the one vault overhead. We carry these pieces in homage, crediting where they come from. The urge to make the cosmos wearable is older and wider than any single culture.

*Nota interna:* ⚠️ **curada/importada**, NO forjada. Copy lo declara y **honra la astronomía maya** con respeto. antü/wanglen anclan la unidad sin apropiarse. **Pendiente: confirmar el nombre "Maya" con Ocin antes del launch.**
