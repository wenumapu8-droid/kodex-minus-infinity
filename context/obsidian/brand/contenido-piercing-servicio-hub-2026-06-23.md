## Contenido — DOWNSIZING (página/sección)
**Eyebrow:** THE RETURN VISIT
**Título:** Downsizing — why your follow-up matters
**Intro:** "Your first jewelry is longer on purpose. Coming back to shorten it is one of the most important steps in healing well."

- **Why it's longer at first:** a fresh piercing swells. We fit initial jewelry with extra length so the swelling has room — forcing a tight fit on a new piercing causes pressure, embedding and pain.
- **Why you must return:** once the swelling settles (usually a few weeks, varies by placement), that extra length becomes a liability. Too-long jewelry snags, gets slept on, and lets the piercing move.
- **What happens if you don't:** migration, crooked healing, angle changes, irritation bumps, embedding, longer healing — and most of these can't be undone without removing and starting over.
- **When:** your piercer tells you; commonly ~2–8 weeks depending on placement. Come in for a check-up even if unsure.
- **Don't DIY:** sterile, correct length, done by your piercer. Downsizing is a **preventive measure, not a cure** for an already-angry piercing.
- CTA: "Book your downsize check" → booking.

## Contenido — JEWELRY UPGRADES (página/sección)
**Eyebrow:** ONCE IT'S HEALED
**Título:** Upgrades — the piece it was always meant to wear
**Intro:** "A healed piercing opens the door: gold, stone, rings, the one-of-one pieces. This is where the ritual becomes adornment."

- **Why wait:** initial jewelry is chosen for healing (smooth implant-grade titanium, internally threaded). Once healed, the channel can take heavier, decorative, gold or organic pieces safely.
- **What opens up:** decorative ends, 14k gold, gemstones, rings/clickers, and — for stretched lobes — our organic/fossil pieces.
- **Professional change:** we swap it for you (a clean "jewelry change" service), or guide you to insert safely.
- Tie-in: link to the shop (Shop by Piercing → your placement) + the configurator ("design your own").
- CTA: "Book a jewelry change" → booking.
