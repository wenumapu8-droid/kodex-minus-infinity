# World UFO Day — three ways to read the sky

Today, the 2nd of July, is **World UFO Day** — the day a large part of the planet agrees, if only for one night, to look up and wonder what else is out there. We are a brand named for the sky. Our name, *Wenu Mapu*, means **the Land Above**. We cannot let this night pass in silence — but we also cannot let it pass carelessly.

So we do the only honest thing a maker rooted in the cosmos can do: we keep three registers apart and we label each one plainly. What is **remembered** (documented culture). What is **told** (folklore). And what we **imagine** (our own fiction). We never let them bleed into one another. The stars deserve better than that, and so do the peoples who read them first.

A note before we begin, because it is the rule this whole piece is built on: **the ancestral sky knowledge of the Mapuche and the Inca is not, and must never be presented as, evidence of extraterrestrials.** It is astronomy, calendar, and cosmology — real human science and belief, documented by scholars. Folding it into a UFO story would be a theft dressed as a compliment. We won't do it.

## Where the date comes from

[DOCUMENTED] World UFO Day was formalized in 2001 by Turkish researcher Haktan Akdoğan, and the 2nd of July marks the anniversary of the 1947 **Roswell incident** — debris the U.S. military first called a "flying disc," then a weather balloon, and only in 1994 admitted came from *Project Mogul*, a classified balloon program to detect Soviet nuclear tests. (A separate tradition marks June 24, Kenneth Arnold's 1947 sighting.) The stated purpose of the day: to raise awareness and press governments to declassify what they hold.

## I · DOCUMENTED CULTURE — how the southern peoples read the sky

**Mapuche cosmos — the Wenu Mapu.** In Mapuche cosmovisión the world is layered; the uppermost layer is the **Wenu Mapu** — *the land above* — where ancestral spirits and deities dwell. Our name comes from it. The sky is named: sun **antü**, moon **küyen**, morning star **wüñelfe**, stars **wangülen** (Canio & Pozo, 2015). The **Meli Wangülen** are the four star-spirits. The **Ngaw** (Pleiades) governs time: its heliacal rise before dawn announces the winter solstice and **We Tripantu** (the new year) ~12 days later, and its first appearance was read to forecast the season's rains.

**Inca cosmos — the river and the dark animals.** The Milky Way was **Mayu / Willka Mayu** — *the sacred river* — mirroring the Vilcanota–Urubamba below. The Inca counted bright constellations and **dark constellations**: shapes in the dust-clouds of the Milky Way read as living animals — **Yacana** (llama), **Hanp'atu** (toad), **Yutu** (tinamou), **Atoq** (fox), **Mach'acuay** (serpent). The Pleiades here were **Qullqa** (*the storehouse*), timing the planting.

Two peoples, one sky, read two different ways — and both watched the Pleiades to start the year. That is the documented wonder. It needs no saucers to be extraordinary.

## II · FOLKLORE — Chile's long conversation with the unknown

**The sober record.** Chile has an official body: the **CEFAA** (*Comité de Estudios de Fenómenos Aéreos Anómalos*), created 1997 under civil aviation (DGAC), continuing a 1968 commission; restructured and renamed **SEFAA** in 2021. Notably, it *avoids* the word "OVNI," works with scientists, studies multi-sensor/multi-witness cases, and refuses to speculate about origin — a UFO agency that will not say "aliens."

**The pilgrimage.** In the Maule region, **San Clemente** calls itself an unofficial "world capital of UFO sightings." Since 2008 the local tourism office and SERNATUR have promoted a *Ruta Ufológica*, whose central site is **El Enladrillado** — a high plateau of enormous tiled volcanic blocks that lore calls an alien landing strip.

**The living myth.** **Isla Friendship** — an island that supposedly appears and disappears off the southern archipelagos, home to tall, pale beings who name themselves after angels and travel aboard the *Mytilus II*. Its famous witness, Ernesto de la Fuente, claimed to have been healed of terminal cancer there; investigators later concluded it was an elaborate fabrication. And yet it persists — which is exactly what folklore does. We tell it as that.

## III · WENU MAPU · SIGNAL — a transmission (fiction)

> **This is fiction. Our fiction — the speculative, poetic voice of the Hidden Sky.** Marked so you always know the difference.

*Transmission · unknown origin · 02.07*

We have always said the geometry was **borrowed from the sky**. Tonight we admit we don't know from whom. Somewhere between the storehouse of stars and the dark animals in the river, a signal has been arriving — too slow to be language, too patterned to be noise. It does not ask to be believed. It asks to be *worn*. Every disc of silver we cut is an antenna the size of a fingertip. The body is the receiver; the adornment is the tuning. *End of transmission.*

## The honest line

The Mapuche and the Inca were extraordinary astronomers, and their sky is theirs — documented, human, not a UFO story. The Chilean UFO tales are folklore, told and retold. And the *Signal* is ours — a fiction, named as one. Three ways to read the sky, kept apart on purpose. That is how you honor all of them at once.

**Küme wün** — may the dawn find you well, still looking up.
