# Provenance

All twelve forms and preview loops in this package were generated procedurally
for this product.

The ZIP contains no copied KodeLife projects, Shadertoy shaders, Reddit GIFs,
paid visual-pack content, third-party textures, photographs, external audio or
font files.

The visual direction belongs to the broad traditions of mathematical
visualization, creative coding, shader art and generative motion design. The
included outputs and equations are original implementations for this pack.
