### 35% SIGNAL
MATERIAL MEMORY, PIERCING FIELD NOTE, ANATOMY IS NOT A TEMPLATE, WHY THIS PLACEMENT WORKS, FROM ORE TO OBJECT, IMPLANT GRADE / DECORATIVE GRADE.

### 25% BELIEF
THE BODY IS NOT A DISPLAY, ORNAMENT IS A LANGUAGE, A PIERCING IS A COORDINATE, SCARCITY IS NOT A COUNTDOWN, A SYMBOL WITHOUT CONTEXT IS ONLY A SHAPE.

### 20% PROOF
THE MAKER’S HAND, ATELIER LOG, FIELD TEST, HEALED CONSTELLATION, ORIGIN RECORD, WHY THIS PIECE COSTS WHAT IT COSTS.

### 15% WORLD
KODEX TRANSMISSION, THE ALMANAC, ARCHIVE RECOVERY, SEVEN NODES, SYMBOL 01, THE NIGHT OF TWO FIRES.

### 5% ACTION
BOOK, MAP YOUR EAR, JOIN THE CIRCLE, ENTER KODEX, COLLECT, RESERVE, COMMISSION.

## Frecuencia recomendada

### Semanal
- 1 Signal
- 1 Belief o Proof
- 3 stories de proceso, materia o archivo
- 1 aparición breve del fundador

### Mensual
- 1 transmisión larga
- 1 dossier material
- 1 historia de objeto
- 1 First Circle dispatch
- 1 capítulo o micro-drop coherente
