## 1. Origin and central intuition

KODEX−∞ began as a visual and interactive world built from Nicolás Ortega / Ocín's recurring interests:

- systems rather than isolated images;
- ancestral and cultural memory;
- geometry as an active language;
- archives that behave like living organisms;
- cosmic, biological and computational structures;
- portals, thresholds, observation and return;
- generative art, shaders, procedural graphics and interactive web experiences;
- a desire to make complex information visible, strange and understandable;
- a desire to produce work that can be studied, shared, downloaded, extended and culturally meaningful.

The project evolved from a neo-ancestral visual language into a broader product thesis:

> KODEX−∞ is a living information system that turns evidence, memory, culture, symbols and user decisions into explorable visual structures.

The project should feel as though the user has discovered an unknown archival instrument—not as though they opened another portfolio, dashboard or landing page.

```text

→ choose OBSERVE or REMEMBER
→ ARCHIVE
→ HEART
```

The two initial routes must produce real differences:

### OBSERVE

- privileges current evidence, comparison and inspection;
- reveals source and uncertainty controls sooner;
- uses precise, externally oriented visual channels;
- final artifact emphasizes relations inspected.

### REMEMBER

- privileges sequence, archive memory, versions and testimony;
- reveals temporal traces and prior states sooner;
- uses sedimentation, echo and path memory;
- final artifact emphasizes relations carried through the session.

Neither path ranks the user or implies psychological diagnosis.

## 10. Epistemic discipline

KODEX can hold scientific, historical, cultural, testimonial, philosophical and mythopoetic material, but their truth status must remain distinguishable.

Claim classes:

```text
OBSERVED
DERIVED
ESTIMATED
PROXY
INTERPRETATION
TESTIMONY
SPECULATION
MYTHOPOETIC
SYNTHETIC
UNKNOWN
```

Examples:

- A documented publication date is `OBSERVED`.
- A count computed from documented records is `DERIVED`.
- A model output is `ESTIMATED` or `SYNTHETIC` depending on its role.
- “Cultural importance” may require explicit indicators and remains a `PROXY`.
- A cosmological text can be held as `MYTHOPOETIC` without being presented as physics.
- A KODEX interpretation is `INTERPRETATION`, not hidden objectivity.

## 11. Contemplative and altered-state direction

The user requested meditation, breathwork, heart pulse and altered-state design as part of the KODEX universe.

The safe and defensible direction is:

### Tier A — public self-guided use

- natural breathing awareness;
- comfortable slow breathing without strain;
- visual pacing with no breath retention;
- body orientation;
- short reflective prompts;
- open monitoring;
- immediate pause, stop and return.

### Tier B — stronger safeguards

- longer immersive sessions;
- more emotionally evocative material;
- eyes-closed audio;
- real biofeedback when a verified sensor exists;
- expectation setting, check-ins and post-session grounding.

### Tier C — not an unattended consumer feature

- high-ventilation breathing;
- prolonged breath holds;
- extreme sensory manipulation;
- deliberate induction of intense altered states.

The canonical heart statement is:

> THE HEART DOES NOT SCORE. IT ORIENTS.

Guided pulse is synthetic and labeled `NOT MEASURED`. Sensor mode is disabled until real hardware, consent, signal quality, privacy and failure behavior exist.

- do not create generic sacred-symbol blends;
