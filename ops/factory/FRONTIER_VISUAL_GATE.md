## Purpose

Prevent KODEX from reaching a technical PASS while remaining below the creator's visual, experiential, narrative or interaction standard.

A fast/low-cost worker may implement bounded work, but it may not declare canonical visual acceptance. Visual convergence and high-ambiguity architecture require a frontier-model review gate.

## Frontier visual audit rubric

Every audit compares the current preview to approved references and records concrete deltas for:

1. composition and focal hierarchy;
2. dominant-object scale / viewport occupation;
3. depth and Z-separation;
4. density vs negative space;
5. typography hierarchy and editorial rhythm;
6. palette roles, contrast and light behavior;
7. motion timescales and transition quality;
8. pointer/touch/keyboard semantic parity;
9. desktop/mobile framing;
10. reduced-motion and fallback equivalence;
11. narrative/semantic alignment;
12. scientific, cultural and provenance boundaries.

Prefer measurable language such as `reduce HUD density ~30%`, `increase focal organism viewport occupation from ~35% to ~55%`, or `add a distinct midground depth plane`, not vague judgments such as `make it cooler`.

## Evidence levels

- `BUILD_PASS` — compiles/builds only.
- `FUNCTIONAL_PASS` — required semantics work.
- `DEVICE_QA_PASS` — target viewport/browser checks pass.
- `FRONTIER_VISUAL_PASS` — frontier audit finds no blocking visual delta.
- `CREATOR_VISUAL_PASS` — Ocín explicitly accepts the visual direction.
- `PROMOTION_READY` — all required gates above are satisfied.

`BUILD_PASS != FRONTIER_VISUAL_PASS`  
`FRONTIER_VISUAL_PASS != CREATOR_VISUAL_PASS`
