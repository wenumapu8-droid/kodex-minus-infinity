- state its exit criterion;
- mark all other ideas `NEXT`, `ARCHIVE` or `REJECT`.

### Tuesday–Thursday

- close, carry or block the work packet explicitly;
- update the handoff and decision log;

- do not open another concept track to avoid confronting unfinished work.
