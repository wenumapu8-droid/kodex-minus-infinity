## North-star outcome

Deliver one coherent, reviewable KODEX−∞ vertical slice that proves a living multi-path system without attempting to build the whole universe.

```text
−∞
→ THRESHOLD
→ INTERACTION FIELD
→ MULTIPLE EMERGENT TRAJECTORIES
→ OPTIONAL HEART / 0 PORTALS
→ RETURN / +∞
→ TRAJECTORY ARTIFACT + SOURCES + RE-ENTRY
```

The first release is finite in corpus and node count, but not reduced to two prewritten routes. Its nodes and relations must recombine according to meaningful user interaction.

## Definition of product completion

The vertical slice is complete only when an external visitor can:

1. enter through one common Threshold;
2. make several interactions whose later consequences are visible;
3. navigate a field with multiple valid exits rather than choose one fixed route;
4. cross or combine at least two semantic worlds;
5. understand that the visual codes belong to one authored language;
6. discover or bypass Heart voluntarily;
7. reach Return without being forced through Heart;
8. inspect sources and uncertainty;
9. receive a trajectory-dependent artifact;
10. use the complete journey on mobile, keyboard and reduced-motion mode;
11. explain what their actions changed between entry and Return.

## Operating constraints

- One primary work packet at a time.
- No new world before the vertical slice passes.
- No new visual family without a named node, source and interaction role.
- No hard-coded `PATH A / PATH B` architecture.
- Representative routes may be used for testing, but the runtime must resolve a larger possibility space.
- No generic AI art replacing Ocín's real lineage.
- No fake telemetry, consciousness score or scientific-looking mysticism.
- No uncontrolled Obsidian or Drive publication.
- No production deployment without `APROBAR DEPLOY`.

Every new idea is classified immediately:

```text
NOW
  required to pass the current milestone

NEXT
  valuable after the vertical slice

ARCHIVE
  meaningful research without current implementation role
```

A new idea enters `NOW` only when it names:

- the milestone;
- the affected node or engine;
- the source or authored work;
- the user consequence;
- the acceptance criterion;
