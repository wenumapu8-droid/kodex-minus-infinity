# FIRST CORPUS SELECTION

Status: `PROPOSED DECISION / HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED`

## Selection criteria

The first KODEX corpus must be:

- real and traceable;
- sufficiently bounded for one vertical slice;
- legally and culturally usable;
- visually and relationally fertile;
- capable of producing different OBSERVE and REMEMBER paths;
- understandable to an external user;
- connected to the project's long-term cultural and commercial position;
- implementable without a large backend or unresolved sensor system.

## Candidate A — KODEX Genesis Archive

### Question

How did KODEX change from a visual language into a living information system?

### Sources

- approved public KODEX documents;
- Git commits and repository files;
- rights-cleared public visual works;
- approved prototype specifications;
- explicit decisions and rejected directions;
- selected generated systems whose bytes and provenance are recovered.

### Strengths

- strongest immediate provenance;
- turns KODEX into its own first living archive;
- demonstrates code, information design, memory and Return together;
- produces a credible case study and launch narrative;
- private conversations can remain outside the corpus;
- low external licensing dependency.

### Risks

- can become self-referential;
- must explain why the evolution matters beyond its creator;
- requires careful distinction between verified repository artifacts and reported packages;
- public statements drawn from older art texts may require epistemic framing.

### Recommendation

`RECOMMENDED FOR V0`.

## Candidate B — Visual Lineage of the Portal

### Question

How have portal, threshold and passage structures appeared across selected cultures, architecture, science and digital art?

### Strengths

- broad cultural relevance;
- visually rich;
- natural Threshold and relation graph.

### Risks

- high rights and cultural-review burden;
- risk of flattening distinct traditions;
- requires substantial external research;
- more difficult to complete quickly and responsibly.

### Recommendation

`FUTURE CURATED EXPERIENCE` after cultural and source protocols are operational.

## Candidate C — Living Information Design Atlas

### Question

How do contemporary information designers transform data into personal, biological, botanical, spatial and cultural forms?

### Strengths

- directly positions KODEX within information design;
- strong educational and professional value;
- could become a public resource.

### Risks

- extensive image rights and citation work;
- must not reproduce copyrighted diagrams;
- could appear derivative unless KODEX's transformations are strong.

### Recommendation

`RESEARCH / PUBLIC METHODS PROJECT`, not first runtime corpus.

## Candidate D — Holography, Geometry and Information

### Question

How can selected models from hyperbolic geometry, holography and quantum information be translated into rigorous interactive visual instruments?

### Strengths

- high originality and intellectual depth;
- powerful computational visualization potential;
- long-term research identity.

### Risks

- high scientific validation burden;
- requires solvers, expert review and substantial computation;
- likely to delay product proof;
- not appropriate for speculative claims disguised as science.

### Recommendation

`LATER RESEARCH BRANCH` after V0.

## Proposed decision

Select **Candidate A — KODEX Genesis Archive** as the first corpus.

## Admission boundary

Included:

- public GitHub records;
- approved public documents;
- approved public artwork and screenshots;
- generated packs only after bytes, creator, date and rights are verified;
- summarized decisions without private chat content.

Excluded:

- raw conversation exports;
- personal information;
- rights-unclear references;
- restricted cultural material;
- unsupported scientific or spiritual claims;
- unverified descriptions of missing files.

## First ingestion batch

1. `kodex-minus-infinity` canonical bootstrap files;
2. verified `wenu-frontend/feature/kodex-depth-engine` files;
3. approved SVG and Design System packs;
4. Spatial Engine and Observe specifications;
5. Impossible Forms and transparent helix after package recovery;
6. selected public Behance KODEX statements with `MYTHOPOETIC` or `TESTIMONY` classification;
7. decision and rejection records.
