# FIRST CORPUS SELECTION

Status: `DECIDED / KODEX GENESIS ARCHIVE SELECTED / CURATION IN PROGRESS`

Decision record: `ADR-0005`  
Decision date: `2026-08-06`

## Selection criteria

The first KODEX corpus must be:

- real and traceable;
- sufficiently bounded for one vertical slice;
- legally and culturally usable;
- visually and relationally fertile;
- capable of producing different path consequences;
- understandable to an external user;
- connected to the project's long-term cultural and commercial position;
- implementable without a large backend or unresolved sensor system.

## Selected corpus — KODEX Genesis Archive

### Governing question

> How did Ocín's unresolved visual codes become KODEX: a living information system with memory, relation and consequence?

### Sources

- approved public KODEX documents;
- Git commits and repository files;
- checksum-verified prototypes and packages;
- creator-reviewed Ocín visual works;
- individually approved Book fragments or images;
- explicit decisions and rejected directions;
- source-linked generated systems whose bytes and provenance are recovered.

### Strengths

- strongest immediate provenance;
- turns KODEX into its own first living archive;
- demonstrates code, information design, memory and Return together;
- connects the creator's actual visual lineage to the current system;
- produces a credible case study and launch narrative;
- private conversations remain outside the corpus;
- low external licensing dependency;
- can produce distinct Memory and Machine/Artifact paths.

### Risks

- can become self-referential unless the human question remains legible;
- must explain why the evolution matters beyond its creator;
- requires strict distinction between verified repository artifacts, archive-verified packages and reported material;
- historical art statements need epistemic framing;
- Ocín and Book materials require asset-level rights, privacy and cultural review.

## Admission boundary

Included:

- public GitHub records;
- approved public documents;
- approved creator-authored artwork and screenshots;
- generated packs only after bytes, creator, date and rights are verified;
- summarized decisions without raw private chat content;
- clearly classified interpretation and mythopoetic writing.

Excluded:

- raw conversation exports;
- personal information and private reflection;
- private photo sessions;
- rights-unclear references;
- restricted cultural material;
- unsupported scientific or spiritual claims;
- unverified descriptions of missing files;
- generic generated imagery used in place of Ocín's actual lineage.
