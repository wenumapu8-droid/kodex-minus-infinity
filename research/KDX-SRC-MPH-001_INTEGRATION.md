## Experimental modules

### KDX.ATLAS (concept, not shipped)

A source reader offering: ORIGINAL (catalogue/link), CONTEXT (time/culture/creator), CLAIMS (attributed versus independently supported), TRANSFORMATION (Ocín original study), EXPERIMENT (interaction), and RIGHTS (clear/unknown/blocked). Use existing source schema and provenance gates; avoid inventing a competing source registry.

### KDX.SIGNAL CONVERGENCE (prototype candidate)

Two synthetic sets of data bars converge from opposite sides. The visible consequence of a deliberate keyboard/touch/click action is a historical reference identifier (initially SRC-151), its title and a direct citation link. The bars encode alignment as an artistic interaction, **not a scientific reading, physical frequency, or consciousness measurement**. The lab does not assign a KODEX letter, route visitor decisions, or persist a canonical JourneyState.

Event contract to evaluate later:

### KDX.READER and KDX.MNEMOSYNE (research only)

Reader: chapter indices, plates, footnotes, source IDs and versioning. Mnemosyne: justified graph edges among researched works and Ocín originals. Neither should become a mandatory new linear scene.
