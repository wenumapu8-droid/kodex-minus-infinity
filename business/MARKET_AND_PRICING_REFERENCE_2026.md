## 2. Licensing implications

Its bundle guidance states that a commercial license may permit up to 5,000 end products/sales under its own marketplace terms; KODEX must write its own simpler and legally reviewed license rather than copying those terms.

## 4. KODEX pricing hypothesis

### Digital asset packs

Do not launch at the marketplace minimum when the pack includes original code, multiple formats, documentation, provenance and commercial-use value.

Initial validation bands:

```text
STARTER / PERSONAL-EDITORIAL       $12–$24
PRO / INDEPENDENT COMMERCIAL       $35–$79
STUDIO / BROADCAST                 $120–$300
EXTENDED / CUSTOM                  quote
```

These are hypotheses for testing, not final prices.

Possible starting test for **Impossible Forms Vol. 1** after validation:

```text
Personal/editorial preview license: $19
Independent commercial license:    $59
Studio license:                     $179
Source/custom adaptation:           quote
```

Do not publish these prices until:

- package bytes and deliverables are verified;
- license terms exist;
- preview quality is strong;
- export formats are validated;
- support boundaries are written;
- comparable current products are reviewed again at launch.

### Services

KODEX's immediate positioning combines creative direction, systems design, AI-context architecture and prototyping. It should not compete as a low-cost production-only freelancer.

Initial internal service bands:

```text
Diagnostic / focused consultation:       $300–$750
Creative-system sprint:                  $1,500–$4,000
Interactive prototype:                   $3,000–$10,000+
Generative identity / launch system:     $5,000–$20,000+
Living archive commission:               $12,000–$50,000+
Institutional installation:              scoped with production partners
Fractional creative direction:           monthly retainer after scope validation
```

These ranges are strategic hypotheses. Exact quotes depend on discovery, rights, research, engineering, revisions, exclusivity, deployment and support.

## 5. Pricing equation

```text
PRICE =
  BASE CREATIVE VALUE
+ RESEARCH AND RIGHTS COMPLEXITY
+ SYSTEM / CODE COMPLEXITY
+ NUMBER OF DELIVERABLE FORMATS
+ LICENSE SCOPE
+ EXCLUSIVITY
+ REVISION AND SUPPORT BURDEN
+ PRODUCTION / PARTNER COST
+ RISK RESERVE
```

Do not price only from hours. Hours remain an internal feasibility and margin check.

## 6. License multiplier model

Provisional internal structure:

```text
PERSONAL / EDITORIAL      1.0×
INDEPENDENT COMMERCIAL    2.5–4×
STUDIO / BROADCAST        6–12×
EXCLUSIVE / BUYOUT        custom; never automatic
```

The multiplier must reflect actual permitted use, seats, audience, redistribution and support.

## 7. Margin protection

For every product calculate:

```text
NET REVENUE
− marketplace/payment fees
− tax reserve
− support time
− update time
− asset hosting
− contractor or music/font licenses
= CONTRIBUTION MARGIN
```

For services calculate:

```text
PROJECT FEE
− research
− production hours
− contractor costs
− software and rendering
− revision reserve
− tax and payment fees
= PROJECT MARGIN
```

## 8. Validation plan

1. publish one free or pay-what-you-want sample;
2. build a waitlist or interest form for the full pack;
3. test two license tiers rather than one low price;
4. measure conversion, refund/support questions and intended use;
5. interview 5–10 designers/studios about deliverables and license clarity;
6. refine price after real evidence;
7. introduce higher tiers only when source, support or studio rights justify them.

## 9. Strategic conclusion

KODEX should occupy the space between cheap motion-background packs and deeply technical production tools.

Its premium is justified when the product includes:

- distinctive authorship;
- original procedural code;
- multiple professional formats;
- transparent/reusable output;
- semantic and visual documentation;
- source or editable tiers;
- commercial licensing clarity;
- a coherent larger universe.

The strongest commercial signal is not quantity. It is **distinctiveness, usability, provenance and a credible system behind the files**.
